1. Executive Summary

Key Finding

Our analysis of 1,938 user reviews across 6 competitor astrology apps reveals that users actively resist paying for AI-generated astrology chatbot features. While general sentiment toward astrology apps is moderately positive (+0.34), the willingness to pay (WTP) for AI chatbot features specifically is strongly negative (-0.33).

-0.33 AI Chatbot WTP

Among the 15 reviews that specifically discuss both AI chatbot features and payment, 40% explicitly stated they are not willing to pay, while only 6.7% showed willingness. The average star rating for these reviews was 1.73 out of 5 — indicating strong dissatisfaction when AI and payment intersect.

Recommendation

Keep the AI astrology chatbot free as a user acquisition and engagement tool. Monetize through premium human-curated content, detailed personalized reports, or advanced features — not the AI chat itself. A freemium model with limited free AI interactions and paid deep-dive features is the safest path.

2. Objective & Business Question

iMeUsWe currently offers a free AI astrology chatbot feature within the app. The business question under evaluation:

Should iMeUsWe make the AI astrology chatbot a paid feature?

To answer this, we needed to understand: (a) how users feel about paid astrology app features in general, and (b) specifically, how they feel about paying for AI-generated astrology chatbot content.

This study was modeled after a similar analysis conducted on Duolingo Max (see Section 8), where the same methodology revealed that users liked AI features but refused to pay for them — a finding that proved accurate when Duolingo's stock corrected.

3. Methodology & Data Sources

3.1 Data Source: Google Play Store Reviews

We scraped user reviews directly from the Google Play Store using the google-play-scraper Python library. This source was chosen because:

  • It contains authentic, unsolicited user opinions (not survey responses)
  • Reviews are publicly available — no API keys or special access required
  • Users naturally discuss pricing, features, and value in app reviews
  • Star ratings provide a quantitative sentiment baseline alongside text

Why not Reddit? Reddit was our initial choice (following the Duolingo study), but Reddit's updated API policies now require a formal registration and policy agreement process. Google Play Store reviews proved to be a richer, more direct data source for app-specific sentiment anyway — users reviewing an app are more likely to discuss payment and features than Reddit commenters.

3.3 Sentiment Scoring

Sentiment scores range from -1.0 (very negative) to +1.0 (very positive). The score combines:

  • Star rating (primary): 1 star = -1.0, 3 stars = 0.0, 5 stars = +1.0
  • Text keywords (secondary): Positive/negative word presence adjusts the score by up to ±0.2

3.4 WTP (Willingness-to-Pay) Scoring

WTP scores range from -1.0 (strongly against paying) to +1.0 (strongly willing to pay). Based on presence of:

  • Positive WTP keywords: "worth it", "would pay", "happy to pay", "good value", "subscribe", "money well spent", etc.
  • Negative WTP keywords: "too expensive", "not worth", "overpriced", "rip off", "should be free", "cancelled subscription", etc.

9. Recommendation for iMeUsWe

❌ Do NOT make the AI astrology chatbot a fully paid feature

The data strongly indicates that users will not pay for AI-generated astrology content. Making it paid risks losing users to free alternatives and generating negative reviews.

✅ Recommended Strategy: Freemium Model

Keep the AI chatbot free as a user acquisition and engagement driver. Monetize through adjacent premium features that users perceive as higher value:

  • Free tier: Basic AI astrology chat (limited daily questions, e.g., 3-5 per day)
  • Paid tier: Detailed birth chart analysis, compatibility deep-dives, personalized monthly forecasts, human-reviewed readings
  • Key principle: Users should feel the free AI chat is generous. The paid tier should offer depth and personalization that feels distinctly premium — not just "more AI."

10. Limitations & Next Steps

10.1 Limitations

  • Sample size for AI+Payment segment: Only 15 reviews specifically discuss both AI chatbot and payment. While the signal is strong and consistent, a larger sample would increase confidence.
  • Google Play Store bias: Reviews skew toward users with strong opinions (very happy or very unhappy). Neutral users are underrepresented.
  • No direct iMeUsWe data: This analysis is based on competitor apps. iMeUsWe's user base may have different characteristics and willingness to pay.
  • Geographic bias: Reviews are primarily from English-speaking markets (US, UK, India). Other markets may differ.
  • Temporal factor: AI sentiment is evolving rapidly. User attitudes toward AI-generated content may shift over time.

10.2 Recommended Next Steps

  1. In-app survey: Run a quick survey within iMeUsWe asking existing users: "Would you pay ₹X/month for unlimited AI astrology chat?" This gives direct signal from your actual user base.
  2. A/B test: Offer a limited-time paid tier to a small user segment and measure conversion rate, retention, and churn.
  3. Monitor competitor moves: Track whether Co-Star, The Pattern, or Nebula adjust their AI pricing strategy in the coming months.
  4. Expand data collection: Scrape Apple App Store reviews (iOS users may have different payment behavior) and Twitter/X discussions for broader sentiment.
